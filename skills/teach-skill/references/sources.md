## Claims not independently verified

- The slides show one-week retention figures of 40% and 61% for a particular comparison, but the original study and measurement conditions were not verified. Do not use them as this skill's effects or expected retention across subjects.
- The summary's statement that retrieval strengthens neural circuits was not independently verified here as a neuroscience claim.
- There are no measured learning outcomes, long-term retention improvements, or exam-pass rates for this package. Conversation behavior checks are not learning-outcome checks.

If the original study or video is provided, compare its title, authors, conditions, measurement time, and claim scope. Do not guess missing sources.

## Operational decisions by the skill author

These choices translate the supplied principles into conversation rules. They are not research-validated optimal teaching methods or direct quotations from the video:

- Keep the ten roles under one entrypoint rather than creating ten separate skills.
- Use a question-and-wait exchange, graduated hints, a different unaided check, and explicit handling of stopping or leaving learning mode.
- Distinguish "hint used," "demonstration seen," and "answered without help," and do not infer mastery from one correct answer.
- Treat recurring-error causes as hypotheses, distinguished by actual solution records and checking tasks.
- Separate source agreement, provisional model-knowledge feedback, and actual external verification.
- Use sample review intervals, self-measured timers, and copyable checkpoints. This package is not a storage, reminder, or timing service.

Consult this reference for source/effectiveness questions or rule changes. It is not required reading in every learning turn and is not an external skill dependency.
